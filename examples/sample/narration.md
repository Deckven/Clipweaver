Edward's crew sails into a storm to finish Maynard's armada, and the Jackdaw trades broadsides with the escort ships until the last one goes down.

Off Anotto Bay the job moves underwater: dive through the reef, mind the breath meter and find the entrance to the smugglers' den.

Back in port the harbourmaster sells the upgrades — armored hull, heavier round shot — paid for in reales, metal and wood.

Shark skins come from the harpoon boat: one bull shark is enough for the next crafting upgrade.
